Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Overview

SubTracker is a privacy-first basketball substitution tracker. All game data is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use cookies to identify you
  • We do not have user accounts or login systems
  • We do not share any data with third parties

Local Data Storage

All team rosters, game data, and substitution logs are saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be cleared by clearing your browser data
  • Is tied to the specific browser and device you use

Advertising (AdSense)

SubTracker may display non-intrusive advertisements via Google AdSense on the History screen. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

Affiliate Links

SubTracker includes affiliate links to products (Amazon, Dick's Sporting Goods). If you click these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. These links are marked with an affiliate disclosure on the Resources screen.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@subtracker.app.

SubTracker is built for youth basketball coaches. No accounts. No cloud. Just basketball.
