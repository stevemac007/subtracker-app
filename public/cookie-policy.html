What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and provide usage information to site owners.

Cookies We Use

SubTracker itself does not use cookies for core functionality — all app data is stored in localStorage. However, when you use our site:

  • Google AdSense may place cookies to serve personalized ads and measure ad performance
  • Analytics cookies may be used to understand how visitors interact with our site

Third-Party Cookies

Google AdSense may set the following types of cookies:

  • Advertising cookies: Used to serve ads relevant to you based on your interests
  • Preferences cookies: Remember your ad settings and opt-outs

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Delete all cookies when you close the browser

To opt out of personalized advertising, visit Google Ad Settings.
