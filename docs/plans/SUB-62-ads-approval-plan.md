# SUB-62 Ads Plan: AdSense Approval

Revision: v1
Owner: CTO
Status: planning

Objective
- Obtain Google AdSense approval guidance and formalize gating for ads in History screen footer.
- Ensure compliance with FTC disclosures and internal policies.

Scope
- In-scope: AdSense banner in History screen footer, gating logic to not show ads during active games, offline handling.
- Out-of-scope: Other ad networks, future ad-free tiers beyond placeholder, non-History screens.

Milestones
- M1: Gather approval requirements from Ads/Legal and Google AdSense policy review (1-2 days).
- M2: Draft plan revision incorporating approval inputs (1 day).
- M3: Create and route confirmation for plan approval (0-1 day once inputs are received).
- M4: Upon confirmation, create implementation subtasks for AdSense integration gating and tests.

Plan Details
- Prepare a concise set of actions to satisfy approval: privacy disclosures, data collection, consent, and ad placement rules.
- Define acceptance criteria for approval: documented sign-off from Ads/Legal and Google policy alignment.
- Define rollback/kill switch if approval is delayed (pause gating changes).

Risks
- Delays in Ads approval could stall implementation.
- FTC disclosures require precise wording; ensure copy consistency.

Next Steps
- Await confirmation to proceed with live plan execution.
