## Channel Evaluation Matrix

### Channel 1: Direct Outreach to Youth Basketball Leagues
**Type**: Outbound B2B2C  
**Effort**: Medium  
**Cost**: Low (time-heavy)  
**Reach**: High within target segment  
**Conversion**: High (league coordinators can onboard dozens of coaches at once)

**Why it works**: A single league coordinator relationship unlocks 8–40 coaches. The flyer is already built for this exact use case. SubTracker's zero-install, offline-capable nature is perfect for youth leagues with varying tech comfort levels.

**Tactics**:
- Compile list of youth basketball associations (Australia-first): Basketball Australia state associations, local junior leagues
- Email campaign with league coordinator flyer as attachment
- Offer free league-wide rollout (all teams free for a season) as a pilot program
- Follow up with phone calls to league offices

**Estimated CAC**: AUD $5–15 per coach (time-based)  
**Time to first users**: 1–2 weeks (pilot league)  
**Scalability**: Medium (requires manual outreach but creates multi-user clusters)

**Priority**: **HIGH** — best leverage of existing flyer asset, highest multi-user potential

---

### Channel 2: Basketball Coaching Forums & Online Communities
**Type**: Community / organic  
**Effort**: Low–Medium  
**Cost**: Free  
**Reach**: Medium  
**Conversion**: Medium (requires authentic participation, not just link-dropping)

**Why it works**: Coaches actively seek tools to improve their game management. Communities are concentrated and engaged.

**Target Communities**:
- Reddit: r/basketballcoaching, r/Coaches, r/Basketball
- Facebook Groups: "Basketball Coaches Network", "Youth Basketball Coaches"
- HoopsU, Breakthrough Basketball forums
- Coach's Clipboard community

**Tactics**:
- Participate authentically in discussions about rotation management and player development
- Share SubTracker as a solution when relevant questions arise
- Post a "tool I built for my coaching" story-style post (not promotional)
- Offer free access in exchange for feedback

**Estimated CAC**: ~AUD $0 (organic)  
**Time to first users**: 1–4 weeks  
**Scalability**: Low–Medium (requires consistent community engagement)

**Priority**: **HIGH** — free, targeted, low barrier to entry

---

### Channel 3: Social Media (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram)
**Type**: Organic + potential paid  
**Effort**: Medium  
**Cost**: Free (organic) / AUD $10–50/day (paid)  
**Reach**: Medium–High  
**Conversion**: Low–Medium

**Why it works**: Basketball coaches are active on Twitter/X (especially during season). LinkedIn reaches league administrators and sports directors. Instagram is visual — good for demo video content.

**Tactics**:
- Twitter/X: Share substitution tips, court time optimization insights, demo video clips. Use #BasketballTwitter #CoachingTwitter
- LinkedIn: Target sports program directors, school athletic directors. Share case-study style posts.
- Instagram: Short-form video (Reels) showing the substitution flow. Link in bio.
- Content cadence: 3–4 posts/week per platform

**Estimated CAC**: AUD $0–20 per user (depending on paid vs organic)  
**Time to first users**: 2–6 weeks (building audience takes time)  
**Scalability**: High (content compounds over time)

**Priority**: **MEDIUM** — good long-term play but slow initial traction. Demo video must be produced first.

### Channel 4: App Store / Google Play (SUB-43)
**Type**: Platform marketplace  
**Effort**: High (currently being built — SUB-43)  
**Cost**: AUD $99/year (Apple) + $25 one-time (Google)  
**Reach**: High  
**Conversion**: Medium (app store discovery is competitive)

**Why it works**: Coaches trust app stores for finding tools. The wrapper approach (Capacitor) means we can publish without rebuilding. App store SEO for "basketball substitution" keywords could drive steady organic installs.

**Status**: In progress via SUB-43 (iOS + Android wrapper, screenshots, store assets).

**Tactics**:
- Optimize app title/description for keywords: "basketball substitution tracker", "court time tracker"
- Use captured screenshots as app store assets
- Encourage early users to leave reviews
- Cross-promote from web app ("Get the app for offline access")

**Estimated CAC**: AUD $2–8 per user (store cut + acquisition)  
**Time to first users**: 4–8 weeks (after SUB-43 ships)  
**Scalability**: High

**Priority**: **HIGH** (dependent on SUB-43) — essential for long-term credibility and discoverability

### Channel 5: Content Marketing / SEO
**Type**: Inbound  
**Effort**: Medium–High  
**Cost**: Low (time)  
**Reach**: High over time  
**Conversion**: Medium

**Why it works**: Coaches search for specific problems: "how to track player minutes", "basketball substitution strategy", "fair playing time calculator". We can rank for these and funnel to SubTracker.

**Tactics**:
- Blog posts targeting long-tail basketball coaching queries
- YouTube shorts demonstrating substitution tips (using demo footage)
- Free tools as lead magnets: "Substitution pattern calculator", "Court time planner"
- SEO-optimized landing page highlighting key value props

**Estimated CAC**: ~AUD $0 (organic)  
**Time to first users**: 2–6 months (SEO takes time)  
**Scalability**: High (compounds over time)

**Priority**: **MEDIUM** — important for long-term growth but not immediate

### Channel 6: Email Outreach to Coach Directories
**Type**: Outbound  
**Effort**: Low–Medium  
**Cost**: Low (email tool + list building)  
**Reach**: Medium  
**Conversion**: Medium

**Why it works**: Basketball associations often publish coach directories. Targeted emails with a clear value prop can drive signups.

**Tactics**:
- Build email list from association directories, tournament websites
- Short, value-first email: "Free tool to track substitutions and court time"
- Include link to demo video and flyer
- A/B test subject lines and CTAs

**Estimated CAC**: AUD $1–5 per user  
**Time to first users**: 1–3 weeks  
**Scalability**: Medium

**Priority**: **MEDIUM** — complements Channel 1 (league outreach)

---

### Channel 7: Partnerships with Basketball Associations
**Type**: B2B Partnership  
**Effort**: High (relationship building)  
**Cost**: Low (may require revenue sharing or free tiers)  
**Reach**: Very High  
**Conversion**: High

**Why it works**: Official endorsement from a state/territory basketball association gives instant credibility and access to thousands of coaches.

**Tactics**:
- Identify target associations: Basketball NSW, Basketball Victoria, Basketball Queensland, etc.
- Pitch as a free tool for their member coaches
- Offer co-branded version or association-specific landing page
- Present at coach education clinics/workshops

**Estimated CAC**: AUD $0–3 per user (at scale)  
**Time to first users**: 1–3 months  
**Scalability**: Very High

**Priority**: **HIGH** — highest potential impact but longest sales cycle

### Channel 9: Grassroots / Local Basketball Networks
**Type**: Word of mouth + local events  
**Effort**: Medium  
**Cost**: Low  
**Reach**: Low–Medium (geographically concentrated)  
**Conversion**: High (peer recommendations)

**Why it works**: Basketball communities are tight-knit. One coach's recommendation carries weight. Local tournaments and clinics are perfect demo opportunities.

**Tactics**:
- Attend local tournaments and demo the tool courtside
- Sponsor a small local league or tournament (flyer distribution)
- Create a referral program ("Get a free team for every coach you refer")
- Partner with basketball academies for tool training

**Estimated CAC**: AUD $0–5 per user  
**Time to first users**: 2–4 weeks  
**Scalability**: Medium (geographic but highly viral within clusters)

**Priority**: **HIGH** — strong fit for early-stage, leverages offline/word-of-mouth

## Recommended Channel Mix (80/20 Focus)

For early-stage growth, focus 80% of effort on:

1. **League Coordinator Outreach** — highest multi-user leverage, flyer already exists
2. **Coaching Communities** — free, targeted, immediate start
3. **Grassroots/Word of Mouth** — viral within coach networks

These three channels share a common pattern: **they work through coach-to-coach and coordinator-to-coach trust networks**, which is the most efficient way to acquire users in a niche sports tool category.

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Coach inactivity during off-season | High | Time outreach to pre-season (Feb–Mar for AU winter leagues, Sep–Oct for summer) |
| App store rejection or delay | Medium | Web-first strategy ensures channel 1–3 work independently |
| Low community engagement / spam flagging | Medium | Focus on authentic value-add, not link-dropping |
| Association partnerships move slowly | Medium | Run parallel to direct outreach; don't block on partnerships |
