## Post 2 — Tips-Based (Value-First)
**Best for:** Any coaching group, especially active discussion groups

**Title:** 5 rotation management tips I wish I knew in my first coaching season

**Body:**
```
After a few seasons on the bench, here are the rotation habits that made the biggest difference for my team:

1. Write down your starters BEFORE tip-off — sounds obvious, but I've seen coaches panic and forget who they planned to start.

2. Track court time, not just points — parents notice when their kid hasn't played in 8 minutes. Having actual numbers takes the "you're playing favorites" complaints out of it.

3. Plan your first sub at the 4-minute mark — don't wait until players are exhausted. Proactive subs keep energy high.

4. Keep a substitution log — if a parent asks "how much did my kid play?" you should have the answer in 5 seconds.

5. Use the simplest tool possible — I tried spreadsheets, paper, and eventually built something that runs in my phone's browser. Zero setup, just open and go. That's SubTracker if you want to check it out: https://subtracker.app

What's your go-to rotation system? Would love to compare notes.
```
