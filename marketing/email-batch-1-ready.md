## Email 1: Basketball NSW

**To:** info@bnsw.com.au
**Subject:** Free substitution tool for Basketball NSW coaches
**Body:**

Hi Basketball NSW Team,

I noticed Basketball NSW runs multiple junior leagues across NSW — that's a lot of coaches managing rotations courtside with nothing but a clipboard and memory.

I built SubTracker as a free, zero-install tool specifically for youth basketball coaches:

- No app download — works on any phone or tablet in the browser
- No accounts or sign-ups — open the link and start at tip-off
- Works offline — gym Wi-Fi is never reliable
- Tracks substitutions and court time automatically
- Free for one team; $0.99 AUD per additional team

The entire thing lives in the browser — no player data leaves the coach's device.

You can try it right now at https://subtracker.app — takes about 30 seconds to see how it works.

I'd love to offer Basketball NSW a free pilot — every team in your league gets unlimited teams for the season, no charge. No setup required on your end. If your coaches like it, great. If not, no harm done.

Happy to jump on a quick call if you'd like to see it in action.

Best,
