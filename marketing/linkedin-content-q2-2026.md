## Post 2 — Playing Time Transparency
**Best for:** Thursday morning (8-9 AM)

```
The #1 source of conflict between youth basketball coaches and parents isn't strategy, skill level, or team selection.

It's playing time.

At every level — from NBA rotation debates to U12 parent complaints — "how much has my kid played?" is the question that creates the most friction.

The solution isn't more data. It's one metric: total court time per player.

When coaches can show objective minutes, three things happen:
1. Parent complaints decrease (they want fairness, not favoritism)
2. Player trust increases
3. Coaches make better rotation decisions

SubTracker tracks this automatically — tap out, tap in, done. No app download, no account, works offline in gyms with no Wi-Fi.

```

Why? Because every sports tech product assumes coaches want MORE features.

Each new tool requires: download, account creation, interface learning, assistant buy-in, offline reliability. That's 5 barriers before anyone actually uses it.

The most requested feature from volunteer coaches isn't a new dashboard. It's "something that just works."
