### Week 4: Authority & Thought Leadership Theme

#### Post 13 (Monday) — Thread: What Coaches Get Wrong About Rotations
```
After talking to dozens of youth basketball coaches, I've noticed 3 rotation mistakes that happen in almost every game:

None of them are about strategy. They're all about tracking.

Let me explain: [thread]
```
**Thread continuation 1:**
```
1/ Mistake #1: Subbing in 5 at once.

When your entire bench goes in simultaneously, all 5 are adjusting at the same time. Nobody is in flow.

Better approach: sub 2 at a time. Keep 3 players on court who know what's happening.

This requires knowing exactly who's been in/out.
```
**Thread continuation 2:**
```
2/ Mistake #2: Reactive substitutions.

Waiting until a player is exhausted to sub them out means they've already lost value on the court. Tired kids make bad decisions, miss shots, and lose defensive position.

Plan your subs at time markers, not fatigue signals.
```
**Thread continuation 3:**
```
3/ Mistake #3: No record of actual court time.

"I think everyone played about the same" is not a rotation strategy. It's a guess.

When you can show exact minutes per player, three things happen:
- Parents stop complaining
- Players trust you more
- You make better decisions next game
```
**Thread continuation 4:**
```
4/ All three mistakes have the same root cause: lack of data.

Not fancy analytics. Just one number: how long was each player on the court?

I built a free tool that tracks this automatically. No app, no account, works offline.

subtracker.app
```
