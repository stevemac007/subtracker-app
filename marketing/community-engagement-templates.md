## Post Template 2: Tips-Based (Value-First)

**Best for:** Breakthrough Basketball Forum, Hoop Leaders, r/basketballcoaching

**Title:** 5 rotation management tips I wish I knew in my first coaching season

**Body:**

After a few seasons on the bench, here are the rotation habits that made the biggest difference for my team:

1. **Write down your starters BEFORE tip-off** — sounds obvious, but I've seen coaches panic and forget who they planned to start.

2. **Track court time, not just points** — parents notice when their kid hasn't played in 8 minutes. Having actual numbers takes the "you're playing favorites" complaints out of it.

3. **Plan your first sub at the 4-minute mark** — don't wait until players are exhausted. Proactive subs keep energy high.

4. **Keep a substitution log** — if a parent asks "how much did my kid play?" you should have the answer in 5 seconds.

5. **Use the simplest tool possible** — I tried spreadsheets, paper, and eventually built something that runs in my phone's browser. Zero setup, just open and go. That's SubTracker if you want to check it out: subtracker.app

What's your go-to rotation system? Would love to compare notes.

## Engagement Playbook

### Week 1-2: Value Building (No Promotion)
- Join all 10 target communities
- Comment on 3-5 existing posts per week with genuine advice
- Answer questions related to rotation management, court time, and game management
- Do NOT mention SubTracker yet

### Week 3-4: Soft Introduction
- Post Template 1 (Story) in the top 3 communities
- Post Template 2 (Tips) in forums where it fits naturally
- Respond to any comments within 2 hours

### Week 5+: Ongoing Engagement
- 1 value post per week (tips, insights, discussion)
- 3-5 helpful comments per week
- Share SubTracker only when directly relevant or asked
- Track engagement metrics (upvotes, comments, clicks)

### Do's
- Lead with the problem you solved, not the product
- Share personal coaching experience authentically
- Respond to every comment and question
- Offer to help even if someone uses a different tool
- Customize each post for the specific community

### Don'ts
- Never post the same content across multiple platforms on the same day
- Don't drop links in the first comment — build credibility first
- Don't argue with negative feedback — thank them and move on
- Don't use promotional language ("amazing," "revolutionary," etc.)
- Don't post more than once per week per community
